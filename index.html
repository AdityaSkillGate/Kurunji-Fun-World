Add More Fun to Your Kodaikanal Trip

Discover why we are the top-rated indoor activity destination in the Princess of Hill Stations.

What are the age limits for the park? expand_more
Kurunji Fun World is designed for everyone! We have dedicated zones for kids as young as 3 years, while our thrill rides and VR experiences are popular among teens and adults.
Do we need to book in advance? expand_more
While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend booking your visit in advance during peak tourist seasons (April-June) and long weekends to ensure entry.
Is the park safe for kids? expand_more
Safety is our #1 priority. All attractions meet international safety standards, and our trained staff supervises every zone. We also provide safety gear for high-intensity activities.
