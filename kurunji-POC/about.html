A Vision to Bring Uninterrupted Joy to Kodaikanal

Founded in 2018 by Mr. R. Karthik, Kurunji Fun World was born out of a simple, heartfelt observation: Kodaikanal's unpredictable weather often meant that family holidays were interrupted by sudden rain or extreme cold.

He envisioned a world-class, weather-proof entertainment hub where families, friends, and thrill-seekers could build memories regardless of the season. What started as a small arcade has now blossomed into the region's largest indoor theme park.

"Our goal isn't just to operate rides. It's to craft unforgettable moments of pure joy for every person who walks through our doors. When I see families smiling together, I know we've achieved our vision."
