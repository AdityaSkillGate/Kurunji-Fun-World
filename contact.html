Frequently Asked Questions

help Do I need to book in advance?

Advance booking is not mandatory for regular visits, but highly recommended for large groups (10+ people) to avail special discounts.

help Are there height restrictions for rides?

Yes, for safety reasons, some thrill rides have a minimum height requirement. Please check the signage at each ride.

help Can I bring outside food?

Outside food and drinks are not allowed. However, we have a fully-equipped multi-cuisine food court inside the park!
